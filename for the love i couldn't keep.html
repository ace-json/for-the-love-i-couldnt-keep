I'm sorry.

That doesn't erase the effect it had. I'm not asking you to excuse me. I just hope that someday you'll understand where it came from.

I was never trying to make your life harder. I was trying, sometimes badly, not to lose you.

the things I needed to say

I don't want to rush into another love just because yours is gone.

I'm not closing my doors to anyone. I still believe that someday I'll find love again. I believe there is still a life ahead of me that I haven't seen yet.

But right now, I want to let myself grieve the love that we lost. I want to sit with it instead of immediately trying to replace it. I want to focus on myself first—to heal, to become okay with my own company, and to figure out who I am when I'm not trying to hold on to someone.

I don't know how long that will take. And I don't need to know.

I'm not moving on because I stopped loving you. I'm moving forward because I have to learn how to love myself, too.

04 — Why I need to

I know myself.

I know I'll spend hours looking for the perfect repost or story that might have changed your mind.

I'll check your socials to see if you've already found someone new. I'll wonder if you changed your mind. I'll look for tiny signs that maybe you still think about me.

And every time I do, I'll be putting myself back at the beginning.

So this isn't punishment. It isn't anger. It's me choosing peace.

I need to stop watching from a distance and hoping for something that you may never want again. I need to give myself the space to actually heal.

Please don't read the block as hatred. I could never make myself hate you just to make leaving easier.
